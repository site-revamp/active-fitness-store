# Active Fitness Store – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Active Fitness Store LLC is a UAE-based fitness equipment retailer and distributor established in 2013. The company supplies home and commercial fitness equipment, sporting goods, and wellness products, alongside gym planning, installation, maintenance, and technical support services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Active Fitness Store as both a comprehensive fitness equipment destination and a commercial gym solutions provider. It creates clearer pathways for individual customers and business buyers while improving product discovery and service enquiries.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Customer Segmentation
- Introduced two distinct customer pathways: **Home Fitness** and **Commercial Fitness Solutions**.
- Improved the separation between individual equipment shopping and business-focused gym solutions.
- Made it easier for home gym owners, fitness enthusiasts, and commercial buyers to identify relevant services.
- Addressed the previous lack of clear navigation for the website's different customer groups.

### 2. Improved Product Discovery
- Organised the extensive product catalogue into clearer visual categories.
- Improved the presentation of cardio equipment, strength-training machines, home gym equipment, accessories, and other sporting products.
- Strengthened category visibility and navigation towards relevant store sections.
- Addressed the difficulty of browsing a broad product range without sufficient visual hierarchy.

### 3. Greater Visibility for Commercial and After-Sales Services
- Gave greater prominence to commercial gym planning, equipment supply, and installation services.
- Improved the presentation of maintenance, repairs, technical support, and after-sales assistance.
- Created clearer enquiry pathways for commercial projects and equipment servicing.
- Addressed the previous lack of visibility for services that extend beyond product sales.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more structured retail-focused design that supports the company's extensive product range and commercial positioning.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved section hierarchy to separate customer pathways, product categories, and service information.
- **Navigation:** Made product discovery and access to relevant commercial solutions more straightforward.
- **User Experience:** Reduced confusion between home fitness shopping and commercial equipment enquiries.
- **Conversion Focus:** Improved the visibility of shopping, commercial consultation, and technical support calls to action.
- **Brand Consistency:** Connected retail products, commercial solutions, and after-sales services within one cohesive homepage experience.

---

**Project Focus:** Clearer customer segmentation, easier product discovery, and stronger visibility for commercial gym solutions and after-sales support.
